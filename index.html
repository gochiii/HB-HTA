01 / Why & definition

Why hospital-based HTA?

Hospital decisions about medical technologies require evidence beyond acquisition prices: clinical value, affordability, organizational readiness, equity and real-world performance all matter.

What is HB-HTA?

A multidisciplinary, hospital-contextualized assessment process supporting decisions about adoption, use, optimization and reassessment of health technologies.

How we work

Hospital-led, IMRTA-supported. Hospital research and HTA teams assess local questions; IMRTA develops standards, mentors teams and supports quality assurance.

Decision authority

Existing statutory approval channels remain in place

Hospital directors and DMS leadership make purchasing and service decisions only within their applicable legal powers, delegated authority and funding rules. HTA recommendations do not independently authorize procurement or discontinuation of care.

05 / Pilot question intake

Submit an HTA Question

Have a hospital decision that needs better evidence? Share a short, non-confidential question in English to start a hospital-led scoping discussion with IMRTA.

Pilot · Working draft
Open the HTA question form ↗Opens Google Forms in a new tab.

What to prepare

Your hospital or institute, contact name and role, work email, assessment type, HTA question and decision timeline. Choose pre-adoption assessment, post-adoption reassessment, or “not sure”.

Describe the decision, technology or service, alternatives and main uncertainty. No file uploads are requested.

What happens next

  1. The coordinating team reviews the question and its fit for HB-HTA.
  2. The team aims to arrange a kick-off discussion within one month, subject to capacity and mutual availability.
  3. Hospital and IMRTA teams agree the scope, data needs and feasibility together; assessment proceeds only if feasible.

Data and privacy

Do not include patient names, identifiers or records, tender details, supplier quotations, or confidential procurement information. Submit a non-confidential summary only.

Contact details and responses are stored in Google Forms and a linked Google Sheet for coordinating-team review and follow-up. Do not send local datasets through this form; any later data sharing requires a separately agreed process.

This is a working-draft pilot, not approved DMS policy. Submission does not guarantee assessment, funding, procurement approval or a meeting date.
